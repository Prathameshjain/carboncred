You are continuing development of the CarbonCred project.

========================================
PROJECT CONTEXT
========================================
CarbonCred is a blockchain-based carbon credit verification platform.
Users submit high-level project details and images.
The system independently verifies claims using AI models and
produces explainable environmental impact metrics and decisions.

The ML models (vegetation UNet + solar ResNet) are already trained,
evaluated, and saved. Do NOT modify or retrain them.

========================================
USER INPUTS (ONLY THESE COME FROM USERS)
========================================
Users can provide ONLY the following:

- project_name (string)
- project_type ("vegetation" or "solar")
- project_coordinates (lat/long or polygon, dummy allowed)
- project_area_hectares (float)
- project_cost_lakh_inr (float)
- project_images (satellite or drone images)
- claimed_improvement_pct (float)

Users will NEVER provide technical ML metrics.

========================================
SYSTEM-GENERATED INPUTS (INTERNAL ONLY)
========================================
The system derives these automatically:
- vegetation masks
- NDVI images
- solar probabilities
- IoU, Dice scores
- temporal consistency
- land-use classification

────────────────────────────────────────
2) VEGETATION VERIFICATION LOGIC
────────────────────────────────────────

Compute internal score:
vegetation_score =
    0.6 * mean_iou +
    0.3 * mean_dice +
    0.1 * temporal_consistency

Impact metrics:
- vegetation_coverage_pct
- vegetation_growth_pct
- ndvi_change
- soil_health_index = 1 − NDVI_variance
- estimated_carbon_sequestration:
    5.0 tCO₂ / hectare / year × area × coverage
- aqi_improvement_proxy = coverage × soil_health_index

Claim alignment:
claim_gap = claimed_improvement_pct − vegetation_growth_pct

Decision rules:
- mean_iou < 0.5 → REJECTED
- temporal_consistency < 0.7 → REVIEW_REQUIRED
- claim_gap > 15% → REVIEW_REQUIRED
- else → VERIFIED

────────────────────────────────────────
3) SOLAR VERIFICATION LOGIC
────────────────────────────────────────

Compute impact metrics:
- estimated_panel_area (from segmentation)
- estimated_energy_mwh_year =
    panel_area × 4.5 × 365 × 0.18
- avoided_co2_tco2_year =
    energy × 0.7

Decision rules:
- solar_probability < 0.4 → REJECTED
- land_use_conflict detected → REVIEW_REQUIRED
- claimed_improvement_pct inconsistent with avoided CO2 → REVIEW_REQUIRED
- else → VERIFIED
